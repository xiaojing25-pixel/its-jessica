Beyond Work

The practices that shape my days

Outside of product management, I'm a certified yoga teacher — teaching at local neighborhood locations and continuing a lifelong practice of physical discipline that began on a gymnasium floor in China.

Yoga found me after years of intensive physical training — and it offered something different. Where rhythmic gymnastics demanded outward precision and performance, yoga demands introspective alignment and energetic awareness. The rigor is the same; the direction is inward.

Certified Teacher Active practice
Local Studios Neighborhood classes
Lifelong Movement From gymnast to yogi

The thread connecting rhythmic gymnastics, yoga, and my professional work is discipline — the commitment to showing up, preparing exhaustively, and pursuing alignment in everything I do. Whether I'm mapping a complex enterprise workflow or guiding a class through a sequence, the foundation is the same: total presence, careful attention, and the belief that precision and care produce the best outcomes.
